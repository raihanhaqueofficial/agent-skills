# Trademark policy

**An open-source licence grants rights to code. It grants no rights to a name.**
The MIT licence covering this repository says nothing about names or logos, so
this policy states them separately.

## The marks

"Raihan Haque", "Raihan Haque Official" and the associated visual identity are
marks of Raihan Haque. They are **not** licensed by the MIT licence covering
this repository.

## Allowed without asking

- Truthful, descriptive references: "works with agent-skills", "a fork of
  agent-skills", "based on agent-skills".
- Unmodified copies distributed under the project name.
- Articles, talks, tutorials and reviews that name the project.
- Linking to the project or its site.

## Ask first

- Distributing a **modified** version under the project name or marks.
- Using the marks in a product, company, or domain name.
- Anything implying endorsement, sponsorship, or official affiliation.
- Selling merchandise carrying the marks.

## Codename-safe alternatives

If you have forked and changed this work, rename it. Truthful attribution
("based on agent-skills by Raihan Haque") is always fine and always welcome.
