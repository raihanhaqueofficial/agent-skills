## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-25

### Added
- `token-lean-answers` — output discipline for agents: cut what carries
  nothing, keep what prevents a re-ask.
- `short-form-performance-standards` — researched 2026 benchmarks for Reels,
  Shorts and TikTok: hook windows, length bands, cadence, decision metrics,
  and the monetisation reality of the motivation niche. Every figure dated and
  sourced.
- `html-to-video-local` — deterministic, offline HTML/CSS-to-video rendering
  using local Chromium and ffmpeg. Includes the renderer, a template, a
  determinism lint, and an adapted motion doctrine.
- `web-app-teardown` — understand what a live web app actually runs, via its
  shipped bundle and a Chrome DevTools capture, rather than its marketing
  pages. Includes a written ethics boundary.
- `installing-third-party-repos` — triage a repo by shape, install it the way
  its own docs say, mirror layouts with external links, audit it for
  telemetry, and verify with a real check.
- Compliance documentation: `NOTICE`, `TRADEMARK.md`, `DISCLAIMER.md`,
  `PRIVACY.md`, `SECURITY.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, and an
  automated compliance check.

### Notes
- `html-to-video-local` adapts material from the HyperFrames project
  (Apache-2.0). The licence text travels with it at
  `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`; changes are recorded in `NOTICE`.
