# Short-form performance standards

## When to Use

- Setting or auditing the house standard for any short-form channel (Reels,
  Shorts, TikTok) before producing content.
- Judging a briefing, a hook, or a video against research instead of taste.
- Never produce a short without first applying these unless the user overrides.

Numbers below are researched 2026 benchmarks with dates and sources. Treat
third-party figures as **reported, not measured** — sample sizes vary wildly and
several sources publish "illustrative estimates". Say so when quoting them.

## Hook law (the decision happens in 1.5 seconds)

- Functionally the hook window is **~1.5 s**, technically 3 s. A 1,000-reel
  benchmark found the median retention curve drops **47% in the first 1.5 s**.
- **Healthy = 60%+ retention at 3 s.** Broken reels lose ~70% by then.
- Instagram penalises slow intros harder than TikTok. Land the hook by the
  **1 s** mark; complete the payoff promise by **3 s**.
- Reels that clear the bar share four traits: **motion in frame 1, on-screen
  text by frame 4, the opener names a stake by word 5**, and no
  "Are you tired of…" / "Have you ever wondered…" openings.

Hook types by retention (OpusClip, 50M ads): **Specific Outcome 45%**,
POV Realism 42%, Unpopular Opinion / Contrarian 38%, Question 28%, then
curiosity gap.

### Hooks specifically in the motivation niche

From 1,900+ viral motivation reels (Aubrium, 22 Oct 2026):

- Mix by type: **bold claims 28%, relatable lines 21%, curiosity gaps 15%**.
- **Relatable lines travelled furthest** — the median hit **52× the creator's
  follower count**.
- **83% show the hook as text on screen.**
- **Shorter went further:** 1–4 words reached **54×**, 5–8 words **40×**, and
  13+ words collapsed to 25–26×. Cut the first line until it breaks, then stop.

## Length

- **21–34 s** averages ~62% completion vs ~48% for videos over a minute, and
  accumulates the most total watch time (OpusClip).
- Under 15 s has the highest completion (~92%) but less watch time.
- A 50-reel test named **12–18 s** the best band for most niches; hook rate was
  flat across 7 s–30 s (61/64/59%), then decayed.
- **Saves** peak on substantive 45–60 s content; **shares** skew to the
  shortest, most rewatchable cuts. Pick the band from the goal.
- Default for a single-idea motivational short: **24–34 s**.

## Pacing (the anti-boring rule)

- **Nothing still for more than 0.6 s.**
- A cut, caption change, zoom or sound shift every **2–3 s** to hold the
  pattern interrupt.
- **Something lands on every key spoken word.**
- Rotate hook and format deliberately — same voice, pacing and B-roll on every
  post makes retention slide even when topics are good.

## Format rotation

Cycle five formats so the feed never looks like one template: **hard truth,
revelation, story arc, discipline, mindset shift**. The first ~30 videos reveal
which resonates via completion rate, save rate and comments.

## Cadence (faceless content)

| Platform | Floor | Ideal | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | 2–3/wk | 4–7/wk (~1/day) | ~1–2/day |
| YouTube Shorts | 3/wk | 3–7/wk | 1–3/day |
| TikTok | — | 1–3/day | 3/day |

- Faceless/pipeline content can run at the **top** of each range because it
  batches; personality-led content must run mid-range.
- One 2026 analysis: channels posting **3 Shorts/day averaged ~3,500 subs/month
  vs ~800 at 1/day** at equal quality. Past ~10 posts/week returns diminish.
- For faceless YouTube **long-form**, 2–3/week earned roughly the same median
  revenue as daily ($920 vs $960) with 30% fewer views — daily is not required.
- **The cadence you can hold for 90 days beats the highest one you can post once.**

## Timing

- Reels: **11:00–13:00** and **18:00–20:00** local.
- Shorts: 12:00–15:00 and 19:00–21:00 local.
- Best long-form window: 14:00–17:00 Thu–Sat.
- **Reply to comments within 30 minutes** of publishing — it signals live
  engagement. Post to the primary platform first, stagger the rest over 24 h.

## Metrics — judge only on these

Three-second hold (hook), completion rate (pacing), saves and shares (worth
sharing), profile visits per reach (curiosity), follows per profile visit
(grid conversion). **Likes are not a decision metric.** Views-per-subscriber and
early-hour performance are the fastest honest feedback loop.

## Monetisation reality for the motivation niche

- **Shorts-feed RPM is typically under $1 per 1,000 views.** Long-form
  motivation estimates run ~$2–$10; a niche RPM table puts motivational &
  self-help at **$4–$8**, versus $10–$15 for personal finance.
- So: **do not plan on ad revenue.** Lead with digital product sales, affiliate,
  brand deals (no follower minimum), and keyword-comment → DM capture that turns
  reach into an owned email list.
- Audience quality beats size for brand deals: a 100k channel at 5% engagement
  outvalues a 500k channel at 0.5%.

## Applying it

Before producing or approving any short, state: intended hook type and word
count, target length band, the pacing rule, the format slot in the rotation,
and the primary metric being optimised. If a deliverable fails these, say
which rule it fails rather than offering taste.
