# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent, argue about the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Assume competence. Ask before concluding someone was careless.
- Critique the code, the claim, or the evidence — never the contributor.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it plainly. Being wrong in public is
  normal here.
- Respect that maintainers have other work and limited time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discrimination or slurs on the basis of identity.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption, or bad-faith argument for its own sake.
- Sexual attention or imagery in a project space.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space representing this
project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through
https://www.raihanhaqueofficial.com. Reports are read and acted on
proportionately: a private word, a comment removed, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban. The maintainer's decision is final.

This is a small project. Enforcement is by the maintainer, without a formal
committee process — and that is stated plainly rather than dressed up.
